Before you post it

  • Every number has to come from a file in the repo. If you cannot point at where it came from, leave it out.
  • No price, market cap, chart or holders. Not on a card, not in the post under it.
  • Never "won". If someone else's words say it, quote them and name them.
  • Say what is not done. A card that claims less than the truth is fine; one that claims more is not.
  • No dashes in place of commas, and no words like thorough, comprehensive or production-ready.
What the four lines are for

Top line is where to go, usually the site. Second line is the state of the thing in lower case, like "built and measured" or "listed 17 september 2026". Headline is the fact, in two short lines, with a full stop on each. Bottom line is the one sentence that stops someone misreading the headline.
