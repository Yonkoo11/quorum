# Making a Quorum card

For anyone posting on behalf of Quorum. No code, no terminal, nothing to install.

## The short version

1. Open `card-maker.html`. Double-click it, or drag it onto Chrome. It opens like a web page.
2. Type into the four boxes on the left. The card redraws as you type.
3. Click **Save the PNG**. It lands in your Downloads, ready to attach to a post.

That is the whole tool. It works offline, nothing is uploaded, and the file is yours to keep.
If the fonts look wrong, you have opened a copy that lost its fonts; ask for the file again.

## What goes in each box

**Top line.** Where someone should go. Almost always `runquorum.site`.

**Second line.** The state of the thing, in lower case. "built and measured", "listed 17 september 2026",
"sibyl labs hackathon, 16 september 2026". It is a caption, not a sentence.

**Headline.** The fact, on two short lines, each ending in a full stop. This is the whole point of the card,
so it has to be true on its own, with no post around it to explain it. Aim for under thirty characters a
line; the tool shrinks the text if you go longer, and a shrunk headline looks weak.

**Bottom line.** One sentence that stops someone misreading the headline. If the headline says a number, this
is where the number's conditions go: what it was measured on, and when.

## The rules that matter more than the design

These are not style preferences. They are the reason anyone believes this account.

- **Every number comes from a file in the repo.** If you cannot say which file, the number does not go on a
  card. Ask before you guess.
- **No price, market cap, chart, holders or anything about the token's value.** Not on the card, not in the
  post under it, not in a reply.
- **Never say Quorum won anything.** If someone else's post says it, quote them and name them.
- **Say what is not done.** A card that claims less than the truth is safe. One that claims more is the only
  kind of mistake that is hard to undo.
- **No dashes standing in for commas.** No "thorough", "comprehensive", "production-ready", "battle-tested".
  Plain words.

## Two things the tool will not do for you

It cannot check whether your number is real, and it cannot tell you whether a claim is fair. Those are the
two things worth asking about before you post. Everything else, the tool handles.

## Where the style comes from

The card is drawn to the same rules as `ART-DIRECTION.md` in this folder: the paper ground, the ink, the one
green, the two typefaces, and the mark of two circles whose overlap is the finding. If a card ever needs to
look different from what this tool makes, that is a change to the brand, not a change to one post.
